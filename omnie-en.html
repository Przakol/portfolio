My name is Adam Luberda

I have been involved in art since I was a young child. I have attended art classes since the age of seven. I graduated from the High School of Fine Arts in Krakow, obtaining the title of graphic designer. Currently, I am studying painting. Fascinated by the distinctiveness of the watercolor technique from other painting media, I decided to explore it deeply. Over the past few years, I have been working on an original recipe for watercolor paints, learning from my own mistakes and searching through the internet and books. Most of the paintings presented on this website are made using my paints. I organize and conduct lectures on watercolor painting and painting technology (paint production and knowledge about pigments). I am inspired by the paintings of Julian Fałat, German expressionists - mainly Emil Nolde, and the Nabis. I have participated in collective exhibitions and competitions, including:

  • 5th International Art Competition NATURE IN COLORS - Krakow, 2015 - 1st prize,
  • 8th National Art and Photography Biennale - Radom, 2016 - honorable mention,
  • National Art Competition The World of My Imagination - Jelenia Góra, 2017 - 3rd prize,
  • Post-plein-air Exhibition Czerwony Klasztor. Szymbark - Dworek Gallery, Tokarnia, 2017,
  • 6th National Competition The World Around Me - Nowy Wiśnicz, 2018 - 3rd prize,
  • Exhibition of the Drawing Studio of the 1st Year of Painting at UKEN in Krakow - ARTzona, Krakow, 2025,
  • NO TITLE - Norwid Cultural Center "Kuźnia", Krakow, 2025.
  • Vertigo (Zawroty głowy) - Dworek Gallery, Tokarnia, 2026

Besides painting, I am interested in: health sciences, cosmetology, chess, Romania, and parrots (privately a single father of two feathered friends). I invite you to follow my work.
